1

What it's for

One place to see, for every SEO client, whether we delivered what they pay for this month and whether anything needs attention. It reads ClickUp, the client's website, Search Console, Google Analytics, Bing and Business Profile. It never posts, edits or changes anything in those tools.

2

The colours

  • {{ m.icon('red') }}Red needs action now: something owed is short or late, or a key fact (scope, owner) is missing.
  • {{ m.icon('amber') }}Amber is at risk or unknown: overdue tasks, waiting on approval, or a data source that isn't connected.
  • {{ m.icon('green') }}Green means everything owed is on track and every source is fresh.

A blank is never treated as fine. If we don't know something, it shows amber or red until someone fills it in.

3

Delivered means verified

  • Blog posts count once the live page loads and can be indexed, not when a ClickUp task closes.
  • Business Profile posts, photos and review replies count once Google shows them on the profile.
  • Monthly reports count once the account manager marks them sent (due by the 7th).
  • Review replies follow a rule rather than a count: every new review answered within 2 business days (1 on Scale and Organic Max).

"—" always means no data, never zero.

4

Finding your way

  • All clients shows every client as a logo tile, worst first. Click one to open its dashboard.
  • A client's dashboard has a section for each channel: Content & SEO, Search Console, Business Profile, Analytics, Bing, plus Tasks & reports, Setup and Notes. They're listed in the side menu.
  • Setup (on each client) is where scope, owners, dates, logo, rules and connections are fixed.
  • Sources and sync shows which data sources are connected and every sync that ran.
5

A simple routine

Every day · 2 minutes

Open All clients, look only at the red tiles, open each and read the top flag. Nudge the named owner.

Every week

Filter by overdue deliverables and waiting on approval: that's what slips quietly.

Each month · by the 7th

Check every client's report is marked sent and last month's blogs and profile posts are verified.

Data refreshes every morning (05:00 UK time). Any page can be refreshed straight away with Sync now.
